# Privacy Policy — Gamelock Overlay

**Last updated: May 2026**

**Developer:** Hannah Studios  

## Data Collection

Gamelock Overlay does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or biometric information of any kind.

## Biometric Authentication

Gamelock Overlay uses Android's native BiometricPrompt API for secure unlock functionality. All biometric authentication is handled entirely by the Android operating system. 
Biometric data is never accessed, stored, or transmitted by this application.

## Local Storage

Gamelock Overlay stores app settings locally on your device using Android's encrypted storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to Hannah Studios or any third party.

## Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact Hannah Studios via the Google Play Store listing.
